`main.py` writes two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (293 characters, including the line breaks):

> A traffic jam is a zipper stuck halfway, with every car a tooth waiting for the one in front to move before it can slide forward.
> The zipper shows why one stopped car freezes the whole line, but it leaves out that drivers can honk, switch lanes, or turn off, while zipper teeth can only wait.

Line 1 explains a traffic jam to a 10-year-old through one concrete, surprising analogy. Line 2 says in one sentence what the analogy maps (one stopped car freezing the whole line) and what it leaves out (drivers can act; zipper teeth cannot).
